Letters to Fighters

A few words can make a child's day perfect as well.

Through Letter.To, you can write a letter of encouragement to a young cancer fighter or survivor from anywhere in the world. Your words can be a reminder that they are seen, supported, and never alone in their journey.

You don’t need to know their story or have the perfect words. Just write something kind, something that might brighten their day, give them a little hope, and be the start of a perfect day for them.
